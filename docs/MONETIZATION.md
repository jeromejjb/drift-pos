# Monetization (no sender fee on P2P)

We do **not** charge the person sending money (no % or fixed fee per send). Other ways to make money on the transaction and the product:

- **Restaurant / dining** – Take a percentage or fixed fee from the **merchant** on “checkout with Drift” (dining). The customer pays the bill; the restaurant pays Drift.
- **Instant transfer** – Offer “instant” withdrawal to bank for a fee; keep “standard” (1–3 days) free. Sender/receiver isn’t charged; the one cashing out pays for speed.
- **Premium / subscription** – Optional subscription for power users (higher limits, analytics, rewards). Revenue is from the subscriber, not per send.
- **Interchange / card revenue** – If we issue cards or run card payments, we earn interchange; no need to add a separate sender fee.
- **B2B / API** – Charge businesses for API access, white-label, or volume. Sender stays free; business pays.

Keeping P2P free for the sender improves adoption; monetize on merchant side, speed (instant transfer), or value-add (premium, B2B).
